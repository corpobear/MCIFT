# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in MCIFT respectful, professional and free from
harassment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive and specific.
- Welcome questions and differing technical views.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Disclose limitations, uncertainty and conflicts of interest.
- Protect private data, security reports and confidential information.
- Accept responsibility, correct mistakes and respect maintainer decisions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, doxxing,
deliberate disclosure of private information and sustained disruptive behavior are not
acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Do not place
sensitive personal information in a public issue. Until a dedicated private conduct
route is confirmed, this is an owner action item and urgent safety concerns should use
GitHub's platform reporting tools.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently limit
participation when behavior threatens people or the project. Enforcement decisions
should be proportionate, documented privately and avoid unnecessary disclosure.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents the
project elsewhere. It is adapted in substance from common open-source community
standards and is provided under the repository documentation licence terms.
